Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy covers The Root Cause uploader, an application used to manage the YouTube channel for The Root Cause.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload and schedule videos to the YouTube channel owned by the operator. It does not access, read, or act on any other channel or account.

What we collect

The app collects no data from viewers or other users. It does not track visitors to this website, and it does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts of any kind.

How access tokens are stored

OAuth tokens issued by Google are stored only on the operator's private server. They are never shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Root Cause uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to therootcausehq@gmail.com.
